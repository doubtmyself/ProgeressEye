# ProgressEye Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-03-06

ProgressEye ("Service") values your privacy and complies with applicable laws. This policy explains what personal data is processed in the Android mobile app and Windows PC agent, why it is used, how long it is retained, and your rights.

## 1. Personal Data We Process

The Service may process the following:

1. Account and identity data
- Google sign-in data: email, display name, Google account identifier (or Firebase UID)
- Service account identifier: Firebase UID

2. Device and usage data
- Service device identifier (`device_id`)
- Device status (online/offline, last seen, app version, platform)
- Monitoring configuration and task metadata (progress regions, labels, thresholds, status)

3. Remote feature data
- PC screenshot image (JPEG) when requested by the user
- Screenshot metadata (URL, timestamp)
- Command data between mobile and PC (for example: screenshot request, monitoring start/stop)

4. Advertising-related data (mobile app)
- As of this policy effective date, the mobile app is operated in global ad-off mode and does not show ads.
- If ads are re-enabled in the future, ad identifiers and ad performance data may be processed through Google AdMob or similar SDKs.
- Ad-related processing follows Google policies and user device settings.

## 2. Purpose of Processing

We process data for:

1. Sign-in, user identification, and account linking
2. Real-time mobile-PC sync (device list, status, monitoring data)
3. Remote screenshot and command features
4. Service operation, incident response, security, and abuse prevention
5. Ad delivery for free-tier operation (mobile app, currently disabled)

## 3. Retention and Deletion

1. Retention principle
- Data is retained as long as needed to provide the Service.
- If laws require retention, data is kept for the legally required period.

2. Account withdrawal and deletion
- On account withdrawal request, data is not deleted immediately; a 7-day grace period is applied.
- After the grace period, account-related personal data and service data are deleted (for example in RTDB/Firestore).
- A 30-day rejoin restriction may apply from the withdrawal request date.
- Electronic records are deleted using secure methods to reduce recovery risk.

## 4. Third Parties and Processors

The Service uses the following providers:

1. Google Firebase
- Firebase Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging
- Purpose: authentication, data sync, file storage, push/realtime delivery

2. Google OAuth
- Purpose: Google account sign-in

3. Google AdMob (mobile app)
- Purpose: ad delivery and measurement (currently ads are disabled; applies if re-enabled)

These providers process data according to their own policies.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- AdMob Policy: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## 5. International Transfer

Due to Firebase/Google Cloud infrastructure, data may be processed on servers outside your country. We manage this in accordance with applicable laws.

## 6. Your Rights

You may request:

1. Access, correction, or deletion of personal data
2. Restriction of processing
3. Withdrawal of consent and sign-out
4. Account withdrawal request (7-day grace period, 30-day rejoin restriction)

## 7. Children's Data

The Service is not intended for children below the legal age. Where required by law, guardian consent may be requested.

## 8. Security Measures

We apply safeguards including:

1. Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
2. Secure token storage using platform security storage
3. Least-privilege access control and operational monitoring

## 9. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated due to legal or service changes. Material updates will be announced in-app or through notice channels.
